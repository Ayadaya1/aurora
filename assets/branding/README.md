# Nord Scan — Aurora icon

Based on the user-provided Nord Scan logo. Prepared with the built-in imagegen
tool, then exported with macOS sips into PNG sizes 86, 108, 128 and 172 for
Aurora and 266 × 266 for the store. Android/iOS icons are unchanged.

- `nord-scan-master.png`: source for exported sizes.
- `nord-scan-store-266.png`: optional store asset.
- `../../aurora/icons/`: launcher icons installed by the RPM.

Prompt: “Preserve the white serif Sigma, four rounded scanner corner brackets
and dark navy/teal-to-turquoise gradient. Remove the wordmark for legibility
at small sizes. Center the artwork inside the central 70% safe area. Opaque
full-bleed square background, sharp outer corners; no white page, no outside
shadow, no mockup, no extra text.”

Aurora applies its own superellipse mask:
https://developer.auroraos.ru/doc/ui_kit/icons
